# Code of Conduct for SoundWave

SoundWave is built by people who share a passion for music, technology, and open-source software. We want this to be a welcoming and respectful place where everyone can contribute, ask questions, share ideas, and learn from each other.

We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, background, or level of experience.

By participating in the SoundWave community, you agree to help keep it friendly, respectful, and inclusive.

## Our Expectations

We ask everyone in the SoundWave community to:

* Treat others with respect, kindness, and professionalism.
* Be open to different opinions, ideas, and approaches.
* Give constructive feedback without making things personal.
* Use clear and respectful language, especially when discussing disagreements.
* Help create an environment where beginners and experienced contributors can participate comfortably.
* Give credit to others for their work and ideas.
* Remember that behind every contribution is a real person who deserves to be treated with respect.

## Share Your Thoughts

Your thoughts and feedback are an important part of SoundWave.

If you have an idea for improving the project, notice something that could be better, or simply have a different approach to a problem, feel free to share it. We welcome constructive criticism and thoughtful discussions.

You don't need to agree with everyone. Healthy discussions and different perspectives can help SoundWave become a better project.

When sharing feedback:

* Explain your reasoning when possible.
* Focus on the idea or implementation, not the person.
* Be open to questions and alternative suggestions.
* Keep discussions constructive and solution-oriented.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in the SoundWave community:

* Harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or hateful language or behavior.
* Deliberately insulting, humiliating, or targeting other community members.
* Sharing sexually explicit, excessively violent, or otherwise inappropriate content.
* Trolling, spamming, or intentionally disrupting discussions.
* Publishing someone's private or personal information without their permission.
* Any other behavior that makes the community feel unsafe, hostile, or unwelcome.

## Reporting a Problem

If you experience or witness behavior that goes against this Code of Conduct, please let the project maintainers know.

You can report an issue privately if you are not comfortable discussing it publicly. We will do our best to listen, understand the situation, and handle reports respectfully and fairly.

Anonymous reports are also welcome where possible.

## What Happens After a Report?

Reports will be taken seriously and reviewed by the project maintainers.

Depending on the situation, consequences may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban from participating in the SoundWave project and its community.

The goal is not to punish people unnecessarily. Our goal is to protect the community and make sure everyone can participate safely and respectfully.

## Keep It Human

Open-source projects are built through collaboration. People will sometimes make mistakes, misunderstand each other, or disagree about how something should be done.

That's okay.

Before responding, take a moment to understand the other person's point of view. Ask questions when something isn't clear, assume good intentions when reasonable, and try to solve problems together.

Let's keep SoundWave a place where people feel comfortable contributing, learning, experimenting, and sharing their ideas.

## Changes to This Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct may be updated from time to time as the SoundWave community grows.

The latest version will always be available in this file.
